Progress bar — when a future stage is already done

The planning bar fills one segment per blueprint section, left‑to‑right, and turns green on completion. Today a finished segment looks the same wherever it sits — so a downstream stage whose gate is satisfied early floats green ahead of where you actually are, with grey gaps before it.

What I'm solving

In PlanStageBar.tsx each segment is coloured purely by sectionStatus() — complete always renders solid green. There's no concept of a current position, so a stage completed out of sequence (Skills auto‑assigned, a repo linked early, a gate met before its turn) is indistinguishable from in‑order progress and looks like a glitch.

The redesigns below all add one idea: a "you are here" position, and a distinct treatment for complete‑but‑ahead segments — "banked early, awaiting its turn" — so it stays green (credit given) without pretending you've progressed that far. Use the switcher to see each design across states.

Assumption: "current" = the first enabled, applicable, in‑progress section (matches currentSection()). Anything complete with an index past it is "ahead."

Scenario
Complete · in sequence
Current · in progress
Banked ahead · done out of order
Locked / upcoming

Recommendation: V3 (checkpoint pills + playhead) is the smallest change that reads unambiguously at the planner's tiny scale — the ring-vs-fill distinction survives a 20px segment, and the playhead answers "where am I" at a glance. V4 is the richest if the planner can spare the vertical room.
